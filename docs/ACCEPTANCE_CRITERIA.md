# Acceptance Criteria — X-Streme Platform

## 1. STM Merchandise Shop Module
* **Scenario: Purchasing Merchandise**
  * **Given** a registered customer has added an official team jersey to their shopping cart[cite: 1],
  * **When** they complete checkout and process payment[cite: 1],
  * **Then** the system generates a downloadable PDF receipt and updates inventory stock levels automatically[cite: 1].

## 2. PitchMaster Team Management Module
* **Scenario: Tactical Lineup Creation**
  * **Given** a Coach is viewing the tactical pitch interface[cite: 1],
  * **When** they drag and drop active players into a 4-3-3 formation[cite: 1],
  * **Then** the starting XI lineup is saved and pushed to the players' daily schedule feed[cite: 1].

* **Scenario: Medical Status Change**
  * **Given** Medical Staff logs an ankle sprain for a player[cite: 1],
  * **When** the injury record is saved[cite: 1],
  * **Then** the system automatically updates the player's status tag to "Injured" on the coach's match planning board while keeping diagnostic details hidden[cite: 1].

## 3. OmniPitch Streaming Module
* **Scenario: Real-Time Audio Commentary Switching**
  * **Given** a Premium user is watching a live match in 1080p[cite: 1],
  * **When** they select Spanish from the audio track menu[cite: 1],
  * **Then** the stream switches to Spanish commentary within 1 second without video interruption[cite: 1].

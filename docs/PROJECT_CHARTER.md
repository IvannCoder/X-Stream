# Project Charter: OmniPitch

## 1. Executive Summary

**Project Name:** OmniPitch  
**Project Manager:** [To be assigned]  
**Date Created:** September 1, 2026  
**Version:** 1.0

OmniPitch is an innovative all-in-one streaming platform designed to provide comprehensive coverage of European football leagues with real-time multi-language commentary features. The platform aims to deliver a unified viewing experience for football enthusiasts across Europe, enabling simultaneous streaming of matches from multiple European football leagues with commentary available in multiple languages.

---

## 2. Project Vision & Purpose

### Vision
To become the premier streaming destination for European football by delivering seamless, multi-language access to all major European football leagues with professional-grade commentary and interactive features.

### Purpose
OmniPitch addresses the fragmented landscape of European football streaming by providing a single, integrated platform where fans can:
- Access live matches from all major European football leagues
- Receive commentary in their preferred language
- Enjoy interactive features and real-time statistics
- Build personalized viewing experiences based on preferred teams and leagues

---

## 3. Business Justification

### Market Opportunity
- **Untapped Market:** Many European football fans lack convenient access to out-of-region league matches
- **Multi-Language Need:** European market diversity requires simultaneous multi-language commentary support
- **Consolidation Gap:** Current solutions force viewers to use multiple platforms or services
- **Growing Digital Consumption:** Sports streaming consumption in Europe is increasing 15-20% annually

### Strategic Benefits
- **Revenue Generation:** Subscription-based model with tiered access levels
- **Market Differentiation:** Unique multi-language commentary infrastructure
- **Competitive Advantage:** Comprehensive league coverage under one platform
- **Brand Recognition:** Position as the go-to source for European football

---

## 4. Project Objectives

### Primary Objectives
1. **Launch Minimum Viable Product (MVP)** with coverage of 5 major European leagues (Premier League, La Liga, Bundesliga, Serie A, Ligue 1)
2. **Implement Multi-Language Support** with commentary in at least 10 European languages
3. **Achieve User-Friendly Interface** that supports seamless streaming and navigation
4. **Establish Robust Backend Infrastructure** supporting 100,000+ concurrent users
5. **Deploy Monetization System** with flexible subscription models

### Secondary Objectives
1. Develop mobile applications for iOS and Android
2. Implement advanced analytics and personalization features
3. Create community features and social integration
4. Establish partnerships with broadcasting rights holders

---

## 5. High-Level Requirements

### Functional Requirements
- Live streaming capability for multiple simultaneous matches
- Multi-language commentary track selection and real-time switching
- User authentication and account management
- Subscription management and payment processing
- Match scheduling, fixtures, and standings display
- Live statistics, player information, and analytics
- Recording and on-demand replay functionality
- Interactive features (polls, predictions, chat)

### Non-Functional Requirements
- **Performance:** Support 100,000+ concurrent users with <2 second latency
- **Availability:** 99.9% uptime during match events
- **Security:** End-to-end encryption, GDPR compliance, secure payment processing
- **Scalability:** Horizontal scaling to support growth up to 1 million concurrent users
- **Accessibility:** WCAG 2.1 AA compliance for users with disabilities
- **Compatibility:** Support for major browsers (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge) and devices

## 13. Risk Overview

### High Priority Risks
1. **Broadcasting Rights Acquisition:** Delays in securing partnerships could delay launch
   - *Mitigation:* Early engagement with rights holders, legal support
   
2. **Scaling Infrastructure:** Unexpected user growth could overwhelm systems
   - *Mitigation:* Robust auto-scaling architecture, load testing

3. **Multi-Language Quality:** Poor commentary quality could damage brand reputation
   - *Mitigation:* Strict quality standards, professional talent acquisition

### Medium Priority Risks
4. **Regulatory Compliance:** Varying privacy/licensing laws across European countries
   - *Mitigation:* Legal compliance framework, geo-blocking capabilities

5. **Market Competition:** Established competitors may counter with aggressive pricing
   - *Mitigation:* Unique features, community building, subscriber loyalty programs

6. **Technical Integration:** Complex streaming across multiple regions and devices
   - *Mitigation:* Thorough testing, redundant systems, technical partnerships

*Detailed Risk Management Plan to be provided separately*
